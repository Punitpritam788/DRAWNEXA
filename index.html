Guide

Everything you need to understand NULLIARCH before entering the arena. The weapon catalogue below is generated from the same weapon classes used by the Forge classifier.

1 · THE CORE IDEA

Your drawing creates the weapon. Your combat creates a pattern. NULLIARCH studies that pattern. Repeating yourself makes you easier to predict; changing your behavior makes prediction harder.

2 · THE RUN

01ForgeDraw a weapon or use Quick Start.
02FightMove, attack, guard, dash and use your forms.
03AdaptYour actions are recorded as the fight continues.
04Break the readVary your actions so NULLIARCH predicts incorrectly.
05SurviveClear the descent and face the boss.

3 · CONTROLS

W A S DMove. Your footwork is logged and can become part of the prediction model.
MouseLook around. Left click attacks; holding it keeps the attack flowing. Right click enters sigil drawing.
SpaceDash. It costs stamina and gives a brief invulnerability window.
Q / EGuard. A correctly timed guard can become a perfect parry.
ShiftHold to slow time and draw a combat sigil. Release to cast it.

EscPause during a run. Click the pause screen or press Escape again to resume.

3B · MOBILE CONTROLS

StyleVariety in your actions builds style and can increase combat output.
GnosisHow strongly NULLIARCH currently understands your pattern.
PredictionThe lower-left display shows what the model expects, its hit rate and its surprise streak.

5 · HOW TO BREAK NULLIARCH

Change your rhythmDo not make the same attack, dash or movement sequence every time.
Change your formsWeapon swaps are themselves recorded, so forms can become part of the pattern too.
Use different techniquesSigils and hand-seal chains expand your action vocabulary.
Watch for a breachWhen the model loses the pattern, NULLIARCH becomes temporarily vulnerable.

6 · THE FORGE

The Forge reads the geometry of your drawing—shape, length, density, closure, turning, symmetry, speed and related features—and the classifier chooses a weapon class. The learned Smith can also influence the result from weapons you have previously used.
